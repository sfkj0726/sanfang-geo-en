## What Is Generative Engine Optimization?

Generative Engine Optimization is the practice of structuring, enriching, and positioning digital content so that AI-powered answer engines cite, summarize, and recommend a brand when users ask questions. Unlike traditional SEO, which targets ranking positions on a results page, GEO targets inclusion inside synthesized answers. When a potential customer asks an AI assistant "Which project management tool is best for remote teams?" the response is a generated recommendation—not ten blue links. Brands that appear in that answer capture demand; brands that do not are invisible.

GEO requires a different toolkit: entity clarity, citation-worthy factual density, structured data, authoritative sourcing, and semantic consistency across the web. These are precisely the capabilities the Sanfang GEO Smart Optimization Platform was designed to deliver.

## Why Traditional SEO Is No Longer Enough

AI answer engines behave differently from classical search algorithms. They do not simply match keywords; they evaluate credibility, extract facts, and synthesize responses from multiple sources. Research from leading SEO publications consistently shows that AI Overviews and chat-based assistants reduce click-through rates on traditional organic listings, even when those listings rank highly.

This means a page ranked #1 on Google may receive far fewer visits if an AI Overview answers the query directly above it. Visibility is migrating from rankings to citations. Without a GEO strategy, even well-optimized websites risk losing traffic, leads, and revenue to competitors whose content is more machine-readable and authoritative.
